Companion animals & people

Screwworm in pets and humans

Screwworm isn't only a livestock problem. Any warm-blooded animal with an open wound can be infested — including dogs, cats, and humans. Human cases (a condition called myiasis) are rare but real, and thousands have been reported in the current regional outbreak.

Pets

The warning signs are the same as in livestock: a wound that worsens, discharge, visible larvae, and discomfort. If you see any of these, contact your veterinarian promptly.

Humans

Infestation usually starts in an existing wound or around body openings. Anyone with a non-healing wound who has been in an affected area should see a clinician.

Who's most at risk

  • People and animals with open wounds or recent surgery.
  • Anyone who spends time near affected livestock or in affected areas.

What to do

  • Pets: call your veterinarian.
  • People: see a healthcare provider.
  • Animal cases: report to your state animal health authority. How to report →
