Our story

Why we started this

The New World Screwworm returned after 60 years. The eradication method works — but detection is the bottleneck. So we decided to build the intelligence layer.

When the screwworm crossed back into the United States in 2026, it was clear the response already had a proven weapon in sterile flies. What it lacked was speed and precision: knowing exactly where the outbreak was, early enough to act. That gap is where we come in.
