## Topic: Sterile Insect Technique

### Arp et al. 2025
**Title:** Tetracycline has no long-term effects on gut physiology and microbiome of the New World Screwworm, *Cochliomyia hominivorax*, which has positive implications for transgenic male-only rearing systems.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/g3journal/jkaf058
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text — https://academic.oup.com/g3journal/article/doi/10.1093/g3journal/jkaf058/8093002
**Notes:** Tetracycline did not significantly harm fitness; microbiome shifts (Ignatzschineria→Providencia) reversed within one generation after removal.

### Arp et al. 2024
**Title:** Doxycycline is a viable alternative to tetracycline for use in insect Tet-Off transgenic sexing systems, as assessed in the blowflies *Cochliomyia hominivorax* and *Lucilia cuprina* (Diptera: Calliphoridae).
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jee/toae023
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text — https://academic.oup.com/jee/article/117/2/650/7613747
**Notes:** Doxycycline effective at much lower dosages (10–50 μg/mL vs 200 μg/mL for tetracycline), potential cost savings.

### Hickner et al. 2024
**Title:** An alternative chicken-based diet for mass-rearing screwworm flies.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jee/toad219
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text — https://academic.oup.com/jee/article/117/1/348/7451116
**Notes:** Chicken-based diet performed comparably to the standard diet with ~18% cost reduction.

### Tietjen et al. 2023
**Title:** Insights into the genetic landscape and presence of *Cochliomyia hominivorax* in the Caribbean.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00436-022-07757-4
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled) — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00436-022-07757-4
**Notes:** Trinidad/Tobago populations show greater genetic diversity than Dominican Republic.

### Arp et al. 2022
**Title:** The microbiome of wild and mass-reared New World Screwworm, *Cochliomyia hominivorax*.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-04828-5
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text (open access) — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-04828-5
**Notes:** Wild flies showed a more diverse microbiome than mass-reared flies; disease-causing bacteria found in wild flies.

### Concha et al. 2020
**Title:** An early female lethal system of the New World screwworm, *Cochliomyia hominivorax*, for biotechnology-enhanced SIT.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s12863-020-00948-x
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text (open access) — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12863-020-00948-x
**Notes:** Describes tetracycline-controlled two-component and "All-in-one" female-lethal transgenic strains.

### Scott et al. 2020
**Title:** Genomic analyses of a livestock pest, the New World screwworm, find potential targets for genetic control programs.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-020-01152-4
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text (open access) — https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-020-01152-4 (also cross-listed under Surveillance topic on source page)
**Notes:** 534 Mb genome assembly, RNA-seq across life stages, 40 genes under positive selection.

### Paulo et al. 2019
**Title:** Specific gene disruption in the major livestock pests *Cochliomyia hominivorax* and *Lucilia cuprina* using CRISPR/Cas9.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1534/g3.119.400544
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text — https://academic.oup.com/g3journal/article/9/9/3045/6026414
**Notes:** CRISPR disruption of yellow and transformer genes.

### Thomas et al. 2018
**Title:** The use of dried bovine hemoglobin and plasma for mass rearing New World Screwworm.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jisesa/iey052
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text (open access, US Gov work) — https://academic.oup.com/jinsectscience/article/doi/10.1093/jisesa/iey052/5033589
**Notes:** Tested hemoglobin/plasma ratios; 80:20 ratio recommended.

### Sagel et al. 2016
**Title:** Managing ammonia emissions from screwworm larval rearing media.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jee/tov291
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled) — https://academic.oup.com/jee/article-lookup/doi/10.1093/jee/tov291
**Notes:** Evaluated additives (bacteria, potassium permanganate, yucca extract) to reduce ammonia; potassium permanganate performed well and showed antimicrobial properties.

### Concha et al. 2016
**Title:** A transgenic male-only strain of the New World Screwworm for an improved control program using the sterile insect technique.

**Notes:** First male-only sexing strains using a tetracycline-repressible female-lethal system.

### Bendele et al. 2016
**Title:** The testes transcriptome of the New World Screwworm, *Cochliomyia hominivorax*.

### Chaudhury et al. 2015 (rearing diet)

### Pitti et al. 2011

## Topic: Surveillance

### Hickner et al. 2023
**Title:** A new formulation of screwworm fly attractant with reduced hazardous chemicals and transport restrictions.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jme/tjad043
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled) — resolved to https://academic.oup.com/jme/article/60/4/631/7133752
**Notes:** Compares original DMDS-based lure (SL-4) with new DMTS-based lure (SL-5); SL-5 caught similar target-fly numbers but more nontarget bycatch.

### Tietjen et al. 2023 (SNP panel)
**Title:** Development of a diagnostic single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) panel for identifying geographic origins of *Cochliomyia hominivorax*, the New World Screwworm.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vetpar.2023.109884
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled, 403 on ScienceDirect) — metadata via Semantic Scholar
**Notes:** Describes a 6-SNP panel distinguishing South America, Inner Caribbean, and Outer Caribbean source populations.

### Tietjen et al. 2022
**Title:** Geographic population genetic structure of the New World Screwworm, *Cochliomyia hominivorax* (Diptera: Calliphoridae), using SNPs.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jme/tjac024
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text — https://academic.oup.com/jme/article/59/3/874/6552259
**Notes:** Identified 12 geographically structured populations; 2016 Florida outbreak samples clustered with Outer Caribbean population, though definitive origin could not be confirmed.

### Altuna et al. 2021
**Title:** New World Screwworm (*Cochliomyia hominivorax*) myiasis in feral swine of Uruguay: One Health and transboundary disease implications.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s13071-020-04499-z
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text (open access) — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13071-020-04499-z
**Notes:** Documented 27/618 (4.36%) infested feral swine in Uruguay over 3 years, more common in dominant adult males, seasonally correlated with temperature.

### Paulo et al. 2021
**Title:** Disruption of the odorant coreceptor Orco impairs foraging and host finding behaviors in the New World Screwworm fly.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-90649-x
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text (open access) — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-90649-x (also PMC8167109)
**Notes:** CRISPR/Cas9 Orco mutant flies showed severely impaired response to food and host odors.

### Hickner et al. 2020
**Title:** Physiological and molecular correlates of the screwworm fly attraction to wound and animal odors.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-77541-w
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text (open access) — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-77541-w (also PMC7695851)
**Notes:** Electroantennography and antennal RNA-Seq revealed sex differences in chemosensory gene expression (27 of 85 DE genes chemosensory-related).

### Dupuis et al. 2018
**Title:** Molecular characterization of the 2016 New World Screwworm (Diptera: Calliphoridae) outbreak in the Florida Keys.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jme/tjy078
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text — https://academic.oup.com/jme/article/55/4/938/4999825
**Notes:** Found virtually no sequence variation among Keys outbreak specimens (single introduction source) but could not pinpoint geographic origin with available markers.

### Zhu et al. 2017
**Title:** Semiochemicals released from five bacteria identified from animal wounds infested by primary screwworms and their effects on fly behavioral activity.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0179090
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text (open access, CC0) — https://journals.plos.org/plosone/doi?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0179090
**Notes:** A synthetic 5-compound blend attracted flies in both lab and field trials.

### Chaudhury et al. 2016
**Title:** Bacterial volatiles attract gravid secondary screwworms (Diptera: Calliphoridae).
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jee/tov390
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled) — https://academic.oup.com/jee/article-lookup/doi/10.1093/jee/tov390
**Notes:** Proteus mirabilis and Providencia rettgeri elicited strongest landing/oviposition responses; 72-hour incubated substrates most effective.

### Chaudhury et al. 2015 (oviposition attractant)
**Title:** Response of *Lucilia sericata* (Diptera: Calliphoridae) to screwworm oviposition attractant.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jme/tjv054
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled) — https://academic.oup.com/jme/article-lookup/doi/10.1093/jme/tjv054
**Notes:** Sheep blowfly responded to the same 5-compound synthetic attractant; sulfur compounds and indole were most important.

### Chaudhury et al. 2014
**Title:** Volatiles from waste larval rearing media attract gravid screwworm flies (Diptera: Calliphoridae) to oviposit.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1603/ME13193
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled) — https://academic.oup.com/jme/article-lookup/doi/10.1603/ME13193
**Notes:** Identified same 5-compound blend; diluted versions (10x, 100x) more effective than undiluted.

### Skoda et al. 2013
**Title:** Inter- and intraspecific identification of the screwworm, *Cochliomyia hominivorax*, using random amplified polymorphic DNA-polymerase chain reaction.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1673/031.013.7601
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved full text — https://academic.oup.com/jinsectscience/article-lookup/doi/10.1673/031.013.7601
**Notes:** 9 of 40 screened primers gave reproducible RAPD profiles distinguishing C. hominivorax from 7 other species; some primers also showed intraspecific/geographic polymorphism.

### Chaudhury et al. 2012
**Title:** Evaluation of artificial larval rearing media waste as oviposition attractant for New World Screwworms (Diptera: Calliphoridae).
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1603/ME11194
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled) — https://academic.oup.com/jme/article-lookup/doi/10.1603/ME11194
**Notes:** Three-day-old waste medium at 10–25% concentration most attractive; cellulose fiber-based waste outperformed gel-based waste.

### Chaudhury et al. 2010
**Title:** Volatiles emitted from eight wound-isolated bacteria differentially attract gravid screwworms (Diptera: Calliphoridae) to oviposit.
**DOI URL:** https://doi.org/10.1093/jmedent/47.3.349
**Retrieval status:** Retrieved abstract only (paywalled) — https://academic.oup.com/jme/article-lookup/doi/10.1093/jmedent/47.3.349
**Notes:** Volatiles from combined bacteria (5 of 8 species) produced significantly higher attraction/oviposition than single species, with 48–72 hour incubation optimal.
