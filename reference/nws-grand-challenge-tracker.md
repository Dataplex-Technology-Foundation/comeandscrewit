### APP-114311 — The University of Texas at Arlington
**Title:** An Integrated Framework for Next-Gen NWS Surveillance: Utilizing Smart Trap Technology, Targeted Lures and Public Reporting Networks
**Progress:** Well-documented. PI is Shouyi Wang, Associate Professor at UTA; project received over $4.5 million from USDA, running through June 2028 with field work in Texas and Mexico. Centers on an AI-powered "smart trap" that selectively retains wild (non-sterile) screwworm flies, especially high-risk females, while releasing sterile flies and non-targets, feeding a networked real-time alert/predictive risk-mapping system for APHIS. Wang expects a prototype ready by spring 2027.
