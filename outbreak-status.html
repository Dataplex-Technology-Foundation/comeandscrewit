Where it stands in the U.S.

First mainland detection: a bovine in Zavala County, Texas — June 3, 2026. Since then, cases have been confirmed across Texas and New Mexico in cattle, sheep, goats, and at least one dog.

Affected counties reported so far: Brewster, Crockett, Edwards, Gillespie, Jim Hogg, La Salle, Lea, Medina, Pecos, Schleicher, Starr, Sutton, Terrell, Tom Green, Uvalde, Val Verde, Zavala.

Trade & economic impact

  • Mexico halted most U.S. livestock imports (June 2026).
  • Canada imposed a 21-day restriction on cattle recently in Texas (June 5, 2026).

The response

USDA sterile-fly releases are underway in Texas, and Texas is moving to accelerate a domestic sterile-fly production facility.
